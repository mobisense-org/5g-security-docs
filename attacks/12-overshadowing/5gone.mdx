## Attack Mechanism

5Gone moves overshadowing into **5G Stand-Alone** and onto the **uplink** — overshadowing the messages a UE sends *to the gNB* during random access and registration, rather than the downlink the UE receives. The attacker aligns to the uplink grant and overshadows Msg3 / the Registration Request as the gNB decodes it.

```
1. Synchronize to the target 5G-SA cell (uplink timing / grant structure)
2. Wait for a victim's random-access / registration attempt
3. Overshadow the uplink message (Msg3 / Registration) at the gNB:
   - Replace it with a crafted pre-auth message
   - Or corrupt it to deny the procedure
4. The gNB acts on the attacker-controlled uplink content
```

Because it targets what the **gNB** receives, a single uplink overshadowing transmitter can affect the random-access channel for the whole cell.

## What It Enables

- **Cell-wide DoS** — overshadowing Msg3 / Registration denies random access to UEs in the cell.
- **LTE downgrade** — push UEs off 5G-SA toward less-protected fallback.
- **SUCI extraction / replay** — manipulate the pre-auth identity exchange to harvest or replay the SUCI.

<Warning>
Uplink overshadowing is harder than downlink: the attacker must align to the uplink grant and reach the gNB's receiver with the right power, which is why this row is rated 2/5 for replicability. As of mid-2026 only traces are public, not a full toolkit.
</Warning>

## RF Signature

`RF AI` — the attack transmits on the uplink during random access, so a passive RF + AI sensor positioned near the gNB can observe overshadowed Msg3/Registration occasions: duplicated/colliding uplink transmissions on PRACH/PUSCH resources with anomalous power and decoding inconsistencies, correlated with registration failures across multiple UEs.

## How It Relates to Other Attacks

- 5G-SA, uplink-focused evolution of [AdaptOver](/attacks/12-overshadowing/adaptover) (LTE, DL+UL) and [SigOver](/attacks/12-overshadowing/sigover) (LTE, DL broadcast) — same ETH Zurich overshadowing lineage.
- Overlaps in outcome with uplink jamming ([PRACH](/attacks/07-dos/prach-jamming) / [RACH Msg1](/attacks/07-dos/rach-msg1-jamming)) but is message-precise rather than blind jamming.
