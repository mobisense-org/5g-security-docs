## Why this is novel

Classical IMSI / SUPI catchers (SUCI-catcher, ToRPEDO, etc.) require either a rogue gNB or an active modification of the UE↔network exchange. SNI5GECT proves a **purely passive third-party** with a USRP B210 can recover the UE identity from pre-authentication uplink messages, with >70% success at 20 m. No fake cell, no modification, no detectable transmission from the attacker during the sniff phase.

## Replication

For the toolkit setup, hardware list, and step-by-step instructions, see the [Pre-Auth Injection page](/attacks/06-pre-auth-injection/sni5gect#replication-notes) — the same B210 + Docker pipeline supports both sniff-only and sniff+inject modes.

## RF Signature (what a passive defender sees)

During the **sniff phase**, SNI5GECT itself emits nothing — undetectable on the air. The injection phase emits short anomalies that are visible (see the Pre-Auth page).
