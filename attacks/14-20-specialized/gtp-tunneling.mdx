## GTP-U Traffic Hijacking (S&P 2025)

Discovered **749,000 publicly accessible GTP hosts** spanning 1,176 service providers in 162 countries. Attackers can remotely disrupt user endpoints or perform off-path traffic hijacking with bandwidth amplification factor up to 170x.

**Not replicable** with lab hardware — requires carrier GTP endpoint access.

## PFCP DoS

Packet Forwarding Control Protocol DoS attacks on core network functions (SMF/UPF). **Testable against your own Open5GS** instance by sending malformed PFCP messages to the UPF.

**Replication:** Send crafted PFCP Session Establishment/Modification requests to Open5GS UPF. Use scapy or custom scripts.
