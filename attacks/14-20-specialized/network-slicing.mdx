## Rogue gNB Slice Manipulation (2025)

A MITM gNodeB exploits protocol weaknesses to manipulate slice allocation. The rogue gNB can forge slice requests, causing QoS degradation, resource contamination, and approximately 80% CPU saturation on the UPF.

**Replication:** Configure multiple slices on Open5GS, then use modified srsRAN gNB to forge NSSAI (Network Slice Selection Assistance Information) in registration requests. Requires PA for OTA operation.

## Slice Isolation Breach

Slice-hopping attacks allow malicious traffic migration across slice boundaries. Rogue slice instantiation and QoS/resource integrity manipulation are also possible when isolation is improperly configured.

**Replication:** Test on your Open5GS with multiple slice configurations. Verify isolation between eMBB and URLLC slices.
