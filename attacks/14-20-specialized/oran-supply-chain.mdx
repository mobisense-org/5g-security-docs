## O-RAN Interface Exploitation

Open RAN increases attack surface due to new interfaces (E2, O1, A1, F1) and multi-vendor supply chain. The NTIA report identifies that disaggregation creates more potential entry points.

**Your advantage:** You have Benetel 550/640 O-RAN RUs. You can test:
- E2 interface between RIC and CU/DU
- F1 interface between CU and DU
- O1 management interface
- Fronthaul traffic between RU and DU

**Tools:** Deploy [5G-Spector](https://github.com/5GSEC/5G-Spector) as an O-RAN xApp for detection. Use [MobiDojo](https://github.com/5GSEC/MobiDojo) for virtual testing first.

## Extra Hardware Needed
- **PTP-capable fronthaul switch** ($2000-5000) for Benetel RU timing
- 10 GbE NIC for fronthaul connectivity
