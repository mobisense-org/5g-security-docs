## Why It's #2

Fake base stations (rogue gNBs / IMSI catchers) remain the backbone of active cellular attacks. In 5G NR, the lack of authentication on broadcast system information (MIB/SIB1) and the pre-authentication RRC layer means a convincing fake cell can still lure UEs. The attacker spoofs broadcast information to redirect victims, or — at the other end of the spectrum — compromises a real carrier-deployed femtocell.

## 5G vs 4G: What Changed

| Aspect | 4G (LTE) | 5G (NR) |
|---|---|---|
| SIB Authentication | None | None (still unprotected) |
| Identity Protection | IMSI in cleartext | SUCI encryption (ECIES) |
| Cell Reselection | Well-known | Same principles apply to NR |
